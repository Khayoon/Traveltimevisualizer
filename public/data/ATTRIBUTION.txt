The GTA and Ontario graphs are derived databases made from OpenStreetMap
Overpass extracts. See provenance.json and the per-dataset metadata files for
queries, timestamps, source hashes, coverage, speed assumptions and processing.
Service roads are excluded. Ontario contains only major road classes; GTA adds
tertiary, residential, unclassified and living streets. These files do not claim
to contain every drivable road in either region.

CONTEXT GEOMETRY
Ontario boundary and lakes: Natural Earth, 1:50 million, public domain.
Source: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
